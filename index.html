How to use the thumbnail downloader

  1. Open the YouTube video you want to get a thumbnail from.
  2. Copy the video URL from your browser or the YouTube share menu.
  3. Paste the link into the field at the top of this page.
  4. Select Preview thumbnails to see the available images.
  5. Choose a resolution and save the image if that option is available.

What is a YouTube thumbnail?

A thumbnail is the preview image associated with a YouTube video. It appears in places such as search results, channel pages, and recommendations, helping viewers understand what a video is about before opening it.

Creators often use thumbnails to communicate a video's topic at a glance. If you reuse an image in a presentation, website, or social post, check that you have permission to use it.

Tips for getting the right image

  • Use the full video URL when possible.
  • Check that the URL points to a specific video rather than a channel or playlist.
  • Choose the largest available image when clarity matters.
  • If a preview is missing, confirm the video link and try again.
