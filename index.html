How to Resize Signature Image to 10KB–20KB (JPG Format)

Most government and banking exam portals require a scanned signature in JPG format with a file size between 10KB and 20KB. The 10 KB — Signature Size preset in this tool is set precisely for the most common lower limit. If the portal accepts up to 20KB, enter 20 in the custom size field.

Signature Size Requirements for Major Exams

  • SSC (CGL, CHSL, GD, MTS): Signature in JPG, 10KB–20KB
  • IBPS / SBI PO & Clerk: Signature in JPG, 10KB–20KB, dimensions approx. 3.5 cm × 1.5 cm
  • Railways (RRB NTPC, ALP, Group D): Signature in JPG, 10KB–40KB
  • UPSC (DAF & Application Form): Signature in JPG, 10KB–300KB

Signature Size in Pixels (10KB to 20KB)

For a 10KB JPEG signature, a scanned image at approximately 140×60 pixels (width × height) is a reliable starting dimension. For a 20KB target, approximately 200×80 pixels at moderate JPEG quality works well. The exact pixel count depends on the complexity and contrast of your signature — darker, more intricate signatures compress to slightly larger file sizes than simple ones.
